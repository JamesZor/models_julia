# Scottish Lower Open-Play Models and Experiments — Correctness Audit

**Date:** 2026-08-24
**Verdict:** **Do not treat the existing model leaderboard or portfolio results as validated.**
**Scope:** `l01`–`l05` and all `r*.jl` files in this directory, cross-checked against the repository OOS pipeline and the cached Scottish Lower `DataStore` schema.

## Executive summary

The underlying idea—fit non-penalty/non-own-goal scoring and recombine independent penalty/own-goal noise—is reasonable. However, the current implementations contain prediction-time reconstruction errors and temporal leakage. The most important result is that the reported recombination/wealth/pxG champions are not being priced from the same model that was fitted.

The two decisive defects are:

1. OOS rows contain team slugs (`home_team`, `away_team`) but no `home_team_id`/`away_team_id`. The custom prediction adapters try to look those strings up in an integer-keyed `team_map`, fail, and silently use zero attack/defence effects.
2. The fitted hierarchical team effects are multiplied by posterior `tau_alpha`/`tau_beta`, but custom prediction adapters omit those scales and use centered raw effects directly whenever a team is mapped.

These defects invalidate absolute model evaluation and portfolio rankings. Existing saved chains may be reusable after correcting extraction, but all OOS latents, books, metrics, and notes must then be rebuilt.

## Blockers

### 1. OOS team effects silently disappear

**Severity:** Critical
**Files:**

- `l03_recombination_models.jl:890-899, 962-971, 1215-1224`
- `l04_recomb_wealth_models.jl:441-450, 559-568`
- `l05_recomb_pxg_models.jl:384-393, 512-521`

The cached `DataStore.matches` schema has `home_team` and `away_team` string columns and no team-ID columns. The adapters use logic equivalent to:

```julia
h_id = get(team_map, row.home_team, -1)
h_idx = get(team_map, h_id, -1)
```

but `team_map` is keyed by integer IDs. This returns `-1`, after which attack and defence vectors are replaced by zeros. The portfolio backtests therefore generally ignored fitted team strength.

**Required fix:** store a `team_name => training_index` map in each `FeatureSet`, or map OOS names using the same team mapping helper as the core feature builder. Unknown promoted/new teams should use an explicit population-level fallback and be counted/reported.

### 2. Prediction omits hierarchical team scales

**Severity:** Critical
**Files:**

- Training: `l03_recombination_models.jl:625-632`, `l04_recomb_wealth_models.jl:314-321`, `l05_recomb_pxg_models.jl:234-241`
- Prediction: `l03_recombination_models.jl:941-944, 1122-1126, 1194-1197`; `l04_recomb_wealth_models.jl:420-423, 527-530`; `l05_recomb_pxg_models.jl:357-360, 474-477`

Training uses:

```julia
alpha = centered(raw_alpha) * tau_alpha
beta  = centered(raw_beta)  * tau_beta
```

Prediction uses only `centered(raw_alpha)` and `centered(raw_beta)`. This is not the fitted posterior predictive model and can inflate team effects by several times.

**Required fix:** extract `tau_alpha` and `tau_beta` draw-wise and multiply centered raw effects before calculating OOS intensities. Add a regression test that reconstructs training-row intensities from a synthetic chain and compares them with the Turing equations.

### 3. pxG conversion model leaks future outcomes

**Severity:** Critical
**Files:** `l01_open_play_feature.jl:193-223`, `l02_open_play_engines.jl:55-62`, `l05_recomb_pxg_models.jl:80-101`

`CleanProxyXGFeature.fit_on` is declared but ignored. `fit_shot_xg` is fitted from all shots in the complete datastore, including `is_goal` outcomes from target and future folds. Historical feature values therefore use future finishing outcomes.

**Required fix:** implement `:history`/`:training` fitting from each split's `history_match_ids`; apply the fitted cell table to history and target shots without refitting. Cache by history-boundary fingerprint rather than globally.

### 4. Wealth features leak future valuations and future scaling

**Severity:** Critical
**Files:** `l04_recomb_wealth_models.jl:43-170, 196-221`; inherited by `l05_recomb_pxg_models.jl`

The player catalogue is built without an as-of date and selects the maximum observed valuation. Wealth normalization is also computed over the complete datastore before boundary filtering. Later valuations and future lineups can therefore affect historical folds.

**Required fix:** use timestamped valuations available before each match/split. Fit fallback and standardization parameters from history only. Missing lineups must remain explicitly missing or carry uncertainty rather than defaulting silently to exact parity.

### 5. League indexing differs between fitting and prediction

**Severity:** High
**Files:**

- Training: `l03_recombination_models.jl:393-415`, `l04_recomb_wealth_models.jl:234-255`, `l05_recomb_pxg_models.jl:145-170`
- Prediction: `l03_recombination_models.jl:902-905, 974-977, 1227-1230`; equivalent logic in `l04` and `l05`

Every training row is assigned league index 1 and `n_leagues=1`. Prediction assigns tournament 57 to index 2, which is out of range, and silently substitutes a zero league effect. Tournament 56 gets the fitted random effect while tournament 57 does not, despite no such distinction in training.

**Required fix:** either fit a real tournament map with two levels or remove the league random effect entirely. Training and prediction must use the identical mapping.

### 6. NegBin extraction does not reconstruct its penalty hierarchy

**Severity:** High
**File:** `l03_recombination_models.jl:648-656, 1122-1150, 1194-1210`

The NegBin model samples `raw_alpha_pen` and `raw_beta_foul`, centers them, and multiplies by `sigma_team_pen`. Extraction looks for derived/nonexistent `alpha_pen_draw`/`beta_pen_foul` chain names and normally falls back to zeros. Referee effects are also not centered during extraction although they are centered in training.

**Required fix:** reconstruct all transformed parameters from their sampled raw variables and scales exactly as in the Turing model.

## Important modelling/data issues

### 7. “Open play” is actually NP-NOG

`l01` removes penalties and own goals but retains goals and shots from corners/free kicks. Rename the target everywhere to **non-penalty/non-own-goal (NP-NOG)** unless set pieces are explicitly identified and removed.

### 8. Missing side attribution defaults to home

**Files:** `l01_open_play_feature.jl:86-110, 242-251`

Missing incident/shot `is_home` values are converted to `true`, biasing home counts and pxG. Unknown sides should be excluded/reconciled and reported.

### 9. Missing scores become 0–0

**File:** `l01_open_play_feature.jl:145-150`

`coalesce(score, 0)` turns unplayed or failed score records into observed zeros. Training builders should reject or mask incomplete matches.

### 10. Unknown referee masks remove the complete penalty observation

**Files:** `l03:675-678`, `l04:364-365`, `l05:295-297`

`ref_mask` multiplies the entire penalty likelihood. A match with no referee should still inform the penalty baseline, home effect, and team effects; only the referee contribution should be zero/marginalized.

### 11. Posterior variance and observation dispersion are inconsistently represented

The pxG/wealth score adapters emit fixed/hardcoded noise assumptions and advertise Dixon–Coles behavior while constructing independent outer-product score matrices (`l04:623-650`, `l05:579-606`). The emitted `rho` is unused. Documentation should not claim Dixon–Coles adjustment until it is actually applied.

### 12. Fixed penalty conversion and own-goal rates are not fold-estimated

The prediction adapters hardcode `0.768` conversion and `0.0276` own goals per side. If these came from the same full Scottish sample, they leak target outcomes. Either justify them as externally fixed priors/constants or estimate them from history within each fold and propagate uncertainty.

## Experiment-runner validity issues

### 13. Existing portfolio/model rankings are invalid pending latent rebuild

All runners evaluating `l03`–`l05` inherit the OOS extraction defects. In particular, the reported `recomb_pxg_wealth_integrated_hl365_hs2` champion and the Layer-2 shift comparison must be rerun after extraction and leakage fixes.

### 14. Artifact selection is ambiguous

`r03`, `r07`, `r10`, `r11` select experiments by prefixes and filesystem order without validating timestamp, git revision, model configuration, folds, or OOS match IDs. Use an explicit artifact ID/manifest and fail if the exact expected artifact is absent.

### 15. Caches are under-keyed

Cached latents/books are keyed mostly by model label. They do not encode experiment artifact hash, datastore snapshot, odds window, markets, commission, or `BookSpec`. Stale books can silently survive model changes.

**Required fix:** key caches by a deterministic hash of experiment ID + data snapshot + odds configuration + book/policy configuration, or disable reuse during validation.

### 16. Models may be compared on different fixtures

Books are built independently and no common-match intersection/equality assertion is enforced. Report match IDs and require identical fixture/market coverage before ranking models.

### 17. `r07` mixes bookmaker odds with exchange commission

`r07_eval_recomb_benchmark.jl:174-231` uses `ds.odds` but applies `PerBetCommission(0.02)`. Label it as a synthetic bookmaker-plus-haircut test, or use actual Betfair summaries consistently.

### 18. Risk metrics contain errors or unsupported conventions

- `r03` and `r07` calculate Calmar only when `m.mdd > 0`, but MDD is represented as negative, so Calmar is always zero.
- Several runners annualize daily-slate Sharpe with `sqrt(35)` without deriving 35 slates/year.
- RQR and training runs do not consistently set random seeds.

### 19. Policy sensitivity is test-set tuning

`r14_trust_sensitivity_sweep.jl` selects trust/risk settings on the same settled 710-match period used for reporting. Split policy development from an untouched final evaluation period or nest policy tuning inside temporal folds.

### 20. Smoke tests are not genuinely OOS and do not fail hard

`r05_smoke_recomb.jl`, `r12_smoke_recomb_wealth.jl`, and `r15_smoke_recomb_pxg.jl` use boundary target/training matches rather than the next prediction step for their advertised OOS checks. Several smoke scripts print success even after convergence/normalization warnings. `r12_smoke_recomb_wealth.jl:105` also accesses `ScoreMatrix.matrices` instead of `.data`.

## Security issue

Database credentials are embedded in:

- `l01_open_play_feature.jl:32`
- `l04_recomb_wealth_models.jl:43-45`

Remove them from source, rotate both credentials, and require `BF_DB_URL` or a credential-free local configuration.

## What appears sound

- The temporal splitter/OOS framework predicts the next dynamics period when used through `Experiments.extract_oos_predictions`.
- Separating NP-NOG goals from penalty/own-goal noise is a defensible modelling decomposition.
- Poisson thinning (`q * lambda_pen`) and convolution with independent Poisson own-goal noise are mathematically valid conditional on the independence and fixed-rate assumptions.
- Core vectorized likelihood blocks are mostly AD-oriented and avoid loops in Turing models.

## Remediation order

1. Rotate/remove credentials.
2. Fix team mapping and exact posterior transformation reconstruction (`tau`, centered/scaled penalty and referee effects).
3. Add extraction parity tests using synthetic chains and known rows.
4. Make pxG and wealth construction fold-causal.
5. Fix league/referee/missing-data semantics.
6. Make artifacts and caches immutable/config-hashed; enforce common evaluation fixtures.
7. Re-extract every OOS latent, rebuild every book, and rerun diagnostics/backtests.
8. Only then revise leaderboards and reconsider the Layer-2 experiment.

## Minimum validation gates before reuse

- Zero unexpected unknown-team mappings per fold; expected newcomers explicitly reported.
- Prediction equations numerically match Turing equations for sampled synthetic parameters.
- Feature perturbation test: adding future matches cannot change an earlier fold's features or predictions.
- Identical match and market IDs across compared models.
- No NaN/Inf score tensors; every draw sums to one within tolerance.
- Fixed seeds and explicit artifact/config hashes in output.
- Untouched final evaluation period after model and policy selection.
