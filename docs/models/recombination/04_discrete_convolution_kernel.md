# 4. Exact Discrete Convolution Kernel

**Module:** `BayesianFootball.Predictions`  
**Kernel File:** `src/predictions/score_computation/recombination.jl`

---

## 🎯 Theory of Discrete Convolution

When a team's total goals are generated by multiple independent components:
$$Y_{\text{total}} = Y_{\text{open}} + Y_{\text{pen}} + Y_{\text{og}}$$
where:
$$Y_{\text{open}} \sim \text{Poisson}(\mu_{\text{goal\_open}}), \quad Y_{\text{pen}} \sim \text{Poisson}(q_{\text{pen}} \cdot \lambda_{\text{pen}}), \quad Y_{\text{og}} \sim \text{Poisson}(\lambda_{\text{og}})$$

The exact probability mass function of the sum of independent Poisson random variables is itself Poisson with parameter equal to the sum of the rates:
$$Y_{\text{total}} \sim \text{Poisson}(\mu_{\text{total}})$$
$$\mu_{\text{total}} = \mu_{\text{goal\_open}} + q_{\text{pen}} \cdot \lambda_{\text{pen}} + \lambda_{\text{og}}$$

---

## 📐 ScoreMatrix Calculation

For each posterior MCMC draw $k \in \{1, \dots, S\}$:

1. **Calculate Expected Marginal Rates:**
   $$\mu_{\text{total}, h}^{(k)} = \kappa_h^{(k)} \cdot \mu_{\text{open}, h}^{(k)} + q_{\text{pen}} \cdot \lambda_{\text{pen}, h}^{(k)} + \lambda_{\text{og}}$$
   $$\mu_{\text{total}, a}^{(k)} = \kappa_a^{(k)} \cdot \mu_{\text{open}, a}^{(k)} + q_{\text{pen}} \cdot \lambda_{\text{pen}, a}^{(k)} + \lambda_{\text{og}}$$

2. **Compute 1D Marginal Probability Vectors:**
   For $g \in \{0, 1, \dots, M-1\}$ (default $M = 12$):
   $$p_h(g) = \frac{\left(\mu_{\text{total}, h}^{(k)}\right)^g e^{-\mu_{\text{total}, h}^{(k)}}}{g!}$$
   $$p_a(g) = \frac{\left(\mu_{\text{total}, a}^{(k)}\right)^g e^{-\mu_{\text{total}, a}^{(k)}}}{g!}$$

3. **Outer Product Joint Score Grid:**
   $$S_{g_h, g_a}^{(k)} = p_h(g_h) \times p_a(g_a)$$

4. **Optional Dixon-Coles Low Score Adjustment:**
   $$S_{g_h, g_a}^{(k)} \leftarrow S_{g_h, g_a}^{(k)} \times \tau_{\text{DC}}(g_h, g_a; \rho)$$

---

## 🔒 The Mathematical Invariant: $\sum M = 1.000000$

All `Predictions.ScoreMatrix` tensors generated by `compute_score_matrix` are normalized over the truncation boundary so that:
$$\sum_{g_h=0}^{M-1} \sum_{g_a=0}^{M-1} S_{g_h, g_a}^{(k)} = 1.000000 \quad \forall k$$

This invariant ensures that downstream market inferences (1X2, Over/Under, Asian Handicap, Both Teams To Score) are rigorously probability-conserving with zero leak.
